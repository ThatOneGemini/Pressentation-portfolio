What I usually do...

Before committing to my education, I usually did commissions for friends and fans on my social networks in my free time. I always loved making funny clips of my recorded gaming videos, and I was also commissioned to do character designs, photo editing, and voice editing.

Now, while studying in this education, I been learning more editing methods while being trained to make UI and UX design process, advanced video and voice editing, more insight on making moving artwork (both in photoshop/after effects and in Live2D) and so much more!
